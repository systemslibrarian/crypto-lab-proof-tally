PRIVATE AGGREGATION, PROVEN

Proof Tally

Verifiable aggregation · Prio3 · VDAF

Split a salary into two shares, send each with part of a fully linear proof, and watch two aggregators accept the total without either reading the report.

WHY IT MATTERS

Private telemetry is useful only if one liar cannot poison the total. Prio3 rejects a malformed report while keeping its measurement split between servers.

00

Privacy is not validity

Secret sharing hides one measurement and still adds it perfectly, even when the measurement is a lie. Prio3 adds a proof to those shares so the servers can jointly answer one narrow question: is this report shaped correctly?

01

One report, two envelopes

Choose a measurement, split it, then let both aggregators contribute a verifier share. Only their combined verifier can accept.

Measurement type

24-bit range: 0 to 16,777,215. Client-side limits are convenience, not the defense.

  1. 1Enter
  2. 2Shard
  3. 3Prepare
  4. 4Collect

02

Collector tally

The protocol result is computed from output shares. The plain sum is computed separately and compared.

03

Make the verifier say no

These controls bypass the friendly input path and attack the real proof preparation code.

Oversized salary

Submit 4,000,000,000 into a 24-bit Sum circuit. The malicious client constructs a malformed encoding directly.

Damaged proof share

Flip one Field64 element after sharding. The salary shares are untouched; proof consistency fails.

Replayed report

Send the identical report twice. Preparation verifies it again and accepts it again; only this lab's nonce registry, outside the VDAF, refuses the duplicate.

TRUST ASSUMPTION

Put both envelopes on one desk

One aggregator sees only a random-looking share. If both aggregators collude, their shares add back to the encoded input.

04

What a valid proof cannot prove

A validity proof checks the circuit statement, not the world outside it.

NEGATIVE CLAIM 01

A well-formed lie still passes

Prio3 checks that a report is in range and correctly shared. It cannot check that the report is true.

NEGATIVE CLAIM 02

A private share can reveal a public total

Prio3 does not add differential privacy. With one accepted report, the aggregate is the report.

05

Pinned evidence

Runtime checks use the same deterministic inputs as the committed unit tests.

Protocol scope and extension seams

Real here

  • Field64 arithmetic and serialization
  • TurboSHAKE128 with domain byte 1
  • BBCGGI19 FLP prove, query, and decide
  • Prio3Count and bounded Prio3Sum

Not this lab

  • DAP transport or HTTP
  • Poplar1 or IDPF
  • SumVec or Histogram
  • Differential privacy on the result
  • More than two aggregators

Extension seams

  • Vector circuits in src/flp/circuit.ts
  • DP after unshard()
  • Additional VDAF instances beside Prio3

Teaching implementation. The arithmetic and proof flow are real and vector-tested. This is not production crypto: it is not constant-time, has no DAP transport, and keeps demonstration keys in browser memory.
